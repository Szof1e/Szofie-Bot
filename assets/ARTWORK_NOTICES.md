# Artwork provenance and rights

The MIT licence in the repository root covers project code, not the media files
under `assets/`. This collection is prepared at the bot maintainer's request from
the existing game artwork. No licence to third-party characters, designs, clips,
trademarks or reference images is asserted. Attribution is not permission.

**Public-distribution review remains required.** The source records describe
some of these works as edits for a private Discord game and do not establish
redistribution permission. Obtain applicable permission or substitute artwork
you can distribute before publishing those files. This notice does not grant
media rights or certify that the images are freely reusable.

## Provenance groups

- `plushies/`: AI-edited themed variants based on a maintainer-supplied handmade
  Android 21 plush photograph. Photograph/plush credit: TrashKitten Plushies,
  November 2022, @Trash_Kitten / trashkittenplushies. The variants are not official
  works of that creator. Android 21 is a Dragon Ball character; relevant character
  and franchise rights remain with their owners. The unedited photograph is not
  bundled.
- `items/`: Android 21 Autonomous Helper illustration based on a supplied figure
  image. Figure/photo and character rights are not established by the code licence.
- `strategic/`: AI-generated/reference-informed military game illustrations.
  Conventional aircraft/ship/tank names and design references remain with their
  respective manufacturers/owners. Deimos success/failure images involve supplied
  Rainbow Six Siege imagery; Ubisoft and applicable artists retain source rights.
  The source tracker image was credited to Nicolas Niño.
- `thor/`: generated fictional Project THOR and interceptor game illustrations;
  these are not engineering diagrams or official military imagery.
- `imperial_star_destroyer/`, `death_star/`, and Star Wars craft in `space/`:
  generated or reference-edited fan illustrations. Star Wars character, vessel,
  model and reference-art rights remain with Lucasfilm/Disney and applicable
  artists. Arquitens variants also use Fantasy Flight Games reference artwork.
- Star Citizen craft in `space/`: Drake Cutlass, Drake Vulture, MISC Prospector
  and Anvil Carrack fan illustrations based on supplied/official references.
  Relevant design/reference rights remain with Cloud Imperium Games.
- `nyx/`: reference-edited fictional satellite stages inspired by Airbus
  SYRACUSE IV. Airbus retains rights in its design/reference illustration.
- `icbm/` and `aa/`: legacy GIFs converted from maintainer-supplied clips. Original
  clip authors and licences were not recorded, so no redistribution grant is
  inferred. `nuke/` contains legacy Android 21 images with unverified source rights.
